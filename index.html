    Torque lab

    Three live experiments. Push a wrench, mesh a gear train and watch a four-stroke engine breathe. Everything below is real mechanical engineering, running in your browser.

    Torque on the bolt0N·m

    T = F × r × sin θ

    TypeReduction

    Ideal gear train, friction ignored.

    1. IntakePiston falls, intake valve opens, fuel-air mixture flows in.
    2. CompressionValves shut, piston rises and squeezes the mixture.
    3. PowerSpark ignites the charge and the expanding gas drives the piston down. This is where torque is made.
    4. ExhaustExhaust valve opens, piston rises and pushes burnt gas out.
