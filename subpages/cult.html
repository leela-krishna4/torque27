Overview

Rhythmic Fusion is a cultural dance performance presented as part of Torque. This non-competitive event celebrates the passion for dance, bringing performers together to showcase talent, coordination and creativity on stage.

Event Highlights

Engaging performances: Talented students adding a cultural spark to the fest.

Perfect blend: Music, movement and expression to entertain the audience.

Creative platform: Exhibit stage presence in front of peers and faculty.

Why Attend?

A celebration of art and expression, without the pressure of competition.
