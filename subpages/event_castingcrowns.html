Casting Crowns

Where precision and speed meet the art of sand casting

06
Register now

Overview

Demonstrate your proficiency in sand casting, a foundational foundry process. Create a flawless sand mold for a given design in the shortest time while following standard practice.

Technical Process

Step 1: A pre-designed 3D pattern is provided.

Step 2: Ensure proper sand compaction and uniformity to avoid defects like blowholes or misruns.

Step 3: Prepare the drag, assemble the cope and close the mold.

Judging Criteria

Mold quality: Precision and uniformity of the sand mold.

Design accuracy: Adherence to the provided 3D pattern.

Time of completion: Finish within the shortest time.
