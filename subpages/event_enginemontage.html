Overview

Explore the intricacies of internal combustion engines and the art of their assembly. Gain practical experience of engine components and their proper assembly, which is crucial for preventing engine failure.

Event Format

Engine assembly: Teams of up to 4 assemble an internal combustion engine under expert supervision.

Judging: Accuracy, precision, adherence to specifications, teamwork and efficiency.

Note: The team that completes assembly and disassembly in the least time wins.
